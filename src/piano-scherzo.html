About the piece

A five-minute scherzo for piano and orchestra, and my first work for full orchestra. It was written as the School of Music's 2026–27 Senior Orchestral Project, for premiere by the Carnegie Mellon Philharmonic with Ziyu Cai as soloist.

Until the premiere, the recording on this page is a MIDI mockup produced from the finished orchestration. The live recording will replace it after March 21, 2027.
