About the piece

My first sustained attempt to compose away from the keyboard. Nearly everything I had written before involved the piano, usually with me playing it; the concertino is for an instrument I do not play, accompanied by five more that I do not play either, with no piano resonance to lean on.

The work runs as a single movement in three connected parts, a shape I took from Prokofiev's First Piano Concerto. Part I is complete; Parts II and III are being written during the 2026–27 year. The recording here is a MIDI mockup of Part I; a performance has not yet been scheduled.
