About the piece

My first sustained attempt to compose away from the keyboard. Nearly everything I had written before involved the piano, usually with me playing it; the concertino is for an instrument I do not play, accompanied by five more that I do not play either, with no piano resonance to lean on.

Movements I and II are complete. The third is being written during the 2026–27 year. The recordings here are MIDI mockups; a performance has not yet been scheduled.
